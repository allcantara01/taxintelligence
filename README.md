# Tax Intelligence

### Enterprise Fiscal Analysis & Audit Platform

**A software engineering case study focused on fiscal data processing, reconciliation, and business intelligence.**

`Flutter` · `Dart` · `Fiscal Data Processing` · `Business Rules` · `Data Validation`

---

## Overview

Tax Intelligence is a fiscal analysis platform designed to support the processing, validation, and reconciliation of Brazilian tax information.

The project explores how complex fiscal workflows can be transformed into structured, traceable, and actionable information.

**Project type:** Private commercial software  
**Repository purpose:** Public technical and visual portfolio  
**Source code:** Proprietary — not publicly available

## The Challenge

Brazilian fiscal operations involve large volumes of structured information, multiple document formats, and complex validation rules.

Manual reconciliation can be time-consuming and difficult to audit.

The engineering challenge is to organize these processes into a system that supports consistency, traceability, and efficient analysis.

## Core Capabilities

- Import and interpretation of fiscal records.
- SPED and EFD data analysis.
- Fiscal document reconciliation.
- ICMS comparison and validation workflows.
- NCM and CEST classification analysis.
- Reporting and business intelligence dashboards.
- Identification of potential fiscal inconsistencies.

*Capabilities represent the project's intended functional scope and ongoing implementation.*

## Engineering Considerations

**Data integrity:** Consistent interpretation and validation of imported fiscal information.

**Traceability:** Clear relationships between source documents, processed records, and analysis results.

**Maintainability:** Separation of interface components, business rules, and data processing.

**Scalability:** Architecture designed with growing datasets and additional fiscal rules in mind.

**Confidentiality:** Protection of business-sensitive information and proprietary source code.
